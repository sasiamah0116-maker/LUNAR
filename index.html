    Put LUNAR in your phone's calendar

    Every day shows its lunar day type, with Evil Days marked ⛔, plus exact New, Quarter and Full Moon times. Works with iPhone, Android (Google Calendar) and Outlook, and keeps itself updated.

    Step-by-step help

    iPhone: tap “iPhone / Apple Calendar”, then Subscribe. LUNAR appears as its own calendar.

    Android: tap “Android / Google Calendar” and confirm Add. Then open the Google Calendar app → Settings → LUNAR → turn on Sync.

    Anything else: copy the link and use “Subscribe to calendar” / “Add calendar from URL”.

    How LUNAR reads the day

    The lunar month has 30 lunar days. A new lunar day begins each time the Moon moves another 12° ahead of the Sun, so lunar days don't line up with midnight. LUNAR reads each calendar date by the lunar day in force at sunrise (06:00 local), the traditional rule. Each lunar day belongs to one of five types, and the empty days (lunar days 4, 9 and 14 of each half of the month) are marked as Evil Days.

    For reflection and tradition. LUNAR does not make scientific predictions.
